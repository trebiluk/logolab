## Wordmark

- **KuliNet** — Outfit ExtraBold, camel **N**

## How child apps inherit

1. **KuliNet corner mark** on every student surface (Settings / About / hub tile chrome).  
2. Hub tiles: **colored firm logo** for that product — not generic Lucide leftovers.  
3. Product themes stay split:  
   - **TechWorks** = Dream dark purple-blue (classroom)  
   - **Baboo** = Stark white+blue — **never** mix with TW Dream  
   - Others = dark Tech Room door OK, with their own accent (see logo grid)  
4. Type: Outfit / Lexend · fat ≥44 · icon + text · Chromebook-safe (≤2 glow).
